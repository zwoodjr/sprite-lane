Fullscreen on iPhone

Safari always shows tabs and the address bar when you open a webpage link. To get a true full-screen game with no Safari chrome:

  1. Tap the Share button (square with ↑) in Safari.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Close Safari and open the Spirit Lane icon from your Home Screen.

That Home Screen icon launches in standalone mode — no tabs, no Safari borders.

Play offline on iPhone

Safari usually will not open a downloaded .html from Files. Use one of these instead:

  1. Best: tap Open Install Tab, then Share → Add to Home Screen (or Add Bookmark). Open that icon with Wi‑Fi off.
  2. Or Share File → Save to Files, then hope Share → Open in Safari works on your iOS version.
