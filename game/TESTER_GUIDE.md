# Wanderer: tester guide (one page)

Thanks for playing. Wanderer is a top-down extraction shooter for iPhone (portrait). You drop into the dust, fight, loot, and leave through an extract pad before the storm. The longer you stay, the more you can carry out, and the more the dust wants you dead.

## Which build you're on

The build stamp (for example `v0.11.0 · dev`) is at the bottom of the **title screen** and the **pause menu**. Settings → About shows it too. Please put it in every report.

## How to send feedback (30 seconds)

1. Tap **SEND FEEDBACK** on the title screen, or **Pause → Feedback** during a run.
2. Pick a type (BUG / BALANCE / LOOKS / IDEA / OTHER), say how fun it felt (1–5), and write a line or two.
3. Tap **SEND**.
   - **iPhone / Xogot:** Mail opens with the report already filled in (build, hero, tier, HP, FPS, settings, recent log). Pick any recipient and send. The text is also copied, so you can paste it into Messages instead. To include a picture, take a screenshot first (side button + volume up) and attach it.
   - **Web build:** your phone's share sheet opens with the screenshot and the text.

Short and often beats long and rare. "Died at window 2 on Smog, didn't see what hit me" is a great report.

## What to test this round

1. **First launch.** Delete the app's data (or use a fresh install). The Mayday prologue should play by itself, return to the menu, and **PLAY — INTO THE HAZE** should start a run right away. Was anything confusing in the first 5 minutes?
2. **New enemy tricks** (from your 2nd run on). Every attack has a red ring first:
   - **Spitters** (Ash-Singer, Glow-Moth Queen) lob acid at where you're standing and leave a puddle.
   - **Burrowers** (Dune-Stalker, Dune-Serpent) dive, tunnel at you, and pop up under a red ring.
   - **Exploders** (Coil-Spider, Void-Spider) run up, stop and blink, then blow up. Kill one while it blinks and the blast hits its friends instead.
   - **Shield-bearers** (Iron-Horse, Fusion-Golem) block hits from the front (flank them). Their shield bash shoves you.
   - **Summoners** (Geist-Alpha, Apocalypse Wraith) call minions in a purple ring. Kill the summoner and the minions crumble.

   Is each one readable in time? Which ones feel unfair?
3. **Bunker (Hangar) pace.** Does the next upgrade always feel 1–3 runs away? Does maxing out feel like a long goal and not a weekend?
4. **Rook** (charge-in launcher). Rush now braces her (half damage during and just after) and heals a little per enemy rammed. Is she still fun? Is she too tanky?
5. **Ascension** (after your first Dustfall extract). Use the A-level stepper on the Dustfall card. Each level stacks a rule and raises the payout. Do the jumps between levels feel smooth?
6. **Comfort on device.** FPS when the screen is crowded, heat and battery over 20 minutes, text size (Settings → Text size), left-hand layout, the notch / Dynamic Island.

## Good to know

- **Greed:** every extract window you skip makes enemies tougher and healing weaker. Leaving early is always a valid choice.
- **Dying** keeps part of your bag (more on higher tiers). Insured gear (Hangar → Insurance Locker) comes back.
- **ADMIN button** (top-right): owner test tools. Runs that use god mode, damage/defense boosts, timer pause or game speed are flagged **ADMIN RUN** and don't count for bests, tier clears or Ascension.

## When something goes wrong

- **Crash or freeze:** reopen the app. You'll be offered to resume or abandon the run. Then send feedback saying what you were doing.
- **Stuck on a wall or off the map:** Pause → Feedback right there. The report includes your position.
